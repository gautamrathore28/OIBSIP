# Segment-specific marketing recommendations

## Champions / High Value
Reward with VIP benefits, early access, loyalty perks and referral invitations; protect service quality.

## Loyal Regulars
Use replenishment reminders, subscriptions or loyalty milestones to increase retention and order value.

## Potential / Growing
Use personalised cross-sell bundles and carefully tested incentives to encourage a second/third purchase.

## At-Risk / Low Engagement
Run win-back campaigns with relevant product recommendations; suppress repeated discounts for customers who do not respond.
